## Learning Path

A practical progression through these **CTF labs**:

1. Start with **LupinOne Lab Setup** to establish the training environment.
2. Continue with **LupinOne Discovery Enumeration** to build a structured enumeration workflow.
3. Move to **LupinOne: From Enum to Root** to connect enumeration findings to privilege escalation.
4. Begin the Breakout sequence with **Breakout Chain**.
5. Study **Breakout Lessons: Brainfuck to Root** for a challenge involving Brainfuck.
6. Continue with **Breakout to Root 4** and **Breakout the Secret** for additional root and discovery scenarios.
7. Explore **Permission Trap: Tarred & Capable** to understand permissions and capabilities.
8. Study **Capability Breakout** for further Linux capability-focused security concepts.
9. Finish with **Hardening the Breakout Box** and **Breakout Hardening Lab** to approach the same security themes from a defensive perspective.

Difficulty levels are not inferred unless explicitly stated in the supplied lab information.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Open a lab using its direct URL.
2. Review the objectives and environment requirements.
3. Use only the supplied CTF environment or systems where you have explicit authorization.
4. Perform enumeration systematically and document findings.
5. Pay attention to permissions, capabilities, files, services, and configuration.
6. Analyze how individual weaknesses can combine into an access chain.
7. For hardening labs, identify controls that reduce unnecessary privileges and attack paths.
8. Record both offensive findings and defensive remediation ideas.
9. Keep all testing within the authorized environment.
